# Changelog

All notable changes to VerZan Check are listed here, newest first. The page footer shows only the current version.

## 1.2.13 (13 November 2026)
- Product Liability Directive date corrected to 8 December 2026, following the corrigendum to Article 2(1) published on 7 May 2026 (OJ L 2026/90364). Corrected in every language.
- Draft machine translations added: Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong), Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Simplified Chinese.
- The page footer now shows the current version only; earlier changes moved to this file.

## 1.2.12 (11 November 2026)
- Language links to draft machine translations in French, German, Spanish and Japanese.
- The footer states the registered jurisdiction.
- The "operator" line says it is most often the "deployer" under the EU AI Act.

## 1.2.11
- The two lines about the draft test vectors state the second-reader result (31 of 50 inputs matched), as the Zenodo record does.

## 1.2.10
- The AIUC-1 sentence says what the 15 October 2026 release added.
- The Product Liability Directive line says "placed on the market or put into service", as the Directive does.

## 1.2.8 and 1.2.9
- The footer no longer calls the company an independent verifier of AI records, welcomes enquiries, and says that any paid work related to this tool will be declared on the page. The DRCF line is removed.

## 1.2.7
- The company name is spelled StS throughout.

## 1.2.6
- Three questions for anyone handed AI records; a link to the draft test vectors.
- The Product Liability Directive lines say what a claimant still has to prove and quote the Directive's own-use definition of manufacturer.
- Page title and preview text set for link previews; a content security rule stops the page loading anything from other websites.

## 1.2.5 (6 November 2026)
